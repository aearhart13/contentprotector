Reporting Steps

  1. Click View ↗ to open the channel on YouTube
  2. Click the three-dot menu (⋮) near the Subscribe button
  3. Select Report user → Impersonation
  4. State they are impersonating {{ current_client.name }} ({{ current_client.handle }})
  5. Come back here, check the box, and click Mark as Reported

For high-priority cases, also submit the formal impersonation complaint form — it reaches YouTube's Trust & Safety team faster.
